“Glue Song” is a love song about finding someone special
and becoming attached to them. beabadoobee sings about experiencing this unexpected
connection with someone that is unlike anything she has ever felt before, using glue as a
metaphor to symbolize the bond she feels to her new partner.

On October 28, 2022, prior to its release, bea performed this song live
for the first time during her concert at the Brooklyn Steel in Brooklyn, New York while
on her Beatopia Tour. She then posted an Instagram 
Reel of her live performance the following day on October 29th, 2022.
The song’s cover art seems to be inspired by the album cover of Kokyuu by Lily Chou-Chou,
as when placed next to each other, there are clear similarities between the two.
